The Universes Theorem

Structural and Geometric Architecture of Prime Numbers

Research Overview

This work proposes a deterministic and geometric modeling of the distribution of prime numbers, moving away from traditional statistical approximations. By structuring the arithmetic space within a unified framework (Universes $U_1, U_2, U_3$), the model demonstrates the emergence of prime structures through collision-balance mechanisms, ensuring absolute precision (0% error).

Conceptual Correspondences

  • $U_1$ Grid: Structured reference space (corresponding to residue classes $6k \pm 1$).
  • Collisions / Nodes: Arithmetic intersection points generating composite integers.
  • Points of Silence: Structural equilibrium positions uncompromised by intersections (corresponding to prime numbers).
  • Status of Number 1: Used as an original geometric index generator without altering unique factorization.
